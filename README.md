# DisableKeyboard

A macOS menu bar app that lets you toggle your keyboard on and off with a single click. Useful for cleaning your keyboard, preventing accidental key presses, or letting your cat walk across your laptop safely.

Press **⌃⌥⌘K** to toggle the lock from the keyboard — it keeps working while the keyboard is locked, so you can get back in even when the display is off and the menu bar is out of reach.
